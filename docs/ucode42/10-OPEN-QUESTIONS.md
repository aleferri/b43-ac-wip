# Open questions

## ISA and semantics

- **Comparisons without outside confirmation.** `js`/`jns` and
  `jdn`/`jdpz`/`jdp`/`jdnz` follow b43-asm's README; OpenFWWF's comments
  confirm only je/jne, unsigned jl/jge/jg and signed jges/jgs.
- **`orx`/`srx` with parameters other than 7,8 immediates.** A plausible
  formula (rotate and insert, extract from B:A), not checked against the
  hardware.
- **`jzx`/`jnzx` with a nonzero second operand.** Never in the blobs nor
  generated by b43-asm: whether the hardware combines B with A is open.
- **Condition register 5 (COND_PSM, 0x50..0x5F).** The main loop tests it
  (`jext 0x50`, `jnext 0x51`, ...) but its source is unknown.
- **The other external conditions** (RX, PHY, ...) are always false in the
  model; the TBTT (0x2C) now follows the TSF (`08`).
- **0x3D and 0x3E** (bits 13 and 14 of the PHY condition register) decide in
  the beacon branch whether the TBTT transmits (`07`); `cond.inc` does not
  name them.
- **How COND_TX_PHYERR is cleared.** On 0x310 the main-loop handler
  (0x0B1C) does not EOI it; the EOI appears only at 0x0609, on a TX path. In
  the model it stays raised and blocks the beacon; on the hardware it does
  not, otherwise no frame would go out after the first error.
- **Depth of the `calls`/`rets` stack** and rotation width of `rl`/`rr`
  (assumed 16 bits).
- **TKIP** is not implemented.
- The four unknown opcodes (0x000, 0x002, 0x070, 0x071).

## TX engine

- The `--tx-engine` model is built on OpenFWWF's sequence: that a write of
  SPR 0x320 with bit 15 set starts the frame, that COND_TX_POWER follows and
  that each event stays raised until the EOI are modelling choices.
- With this model the COND_TX_DONE handler (0x079E) always takes the branch
  that acknowledges the event at 0x07D3 and goes back to the loop, without
  going through the status report at 0x07D4. Whether, and when, the other
  branch is needed on the hardware is not known.
- On the 0x310 family the synthetic sequence did not reach the suspend
  because of the TSF wait at 0x066C: solved with `--psm-mhz` (`08`, `11`).
- That the 0x8000 value of SPR 0x320 starts the beacon's transmission as
  0x8001 starts a queued frame's is a modelling choice: without it the
  beacon never completed and SPR_BRC stayed at 0x21.

## Time

- **PSM clock and cycles per instruction.** Research for a thesis puts it
  between 80 and 200 MHz; the model assumes one instruction per cycle. The
  captures are not enough to narrow it (`11`).
- **A TBTT already past.** When 0x18C is written with an instant already
  gone, the model moves the TBTT to the next multiple of the interval; what
  the hardware does is not checked.

## SHM/SPR map

- 456 direct SHM addresses of D6220 have no name (507 minus 51).
- The `spr.inc` names are from older cores: for corerev 42 they are leads.
- The meaning of [0x2]/[0x3] of the stamping block: the captures never read
  them.
- Where off0 points when the block at 0x0374 reads [0x01,off0] (`07`).
- The bits of PHY register 0x007 that the TX fault handler copies to SHM,
  and the contents of [0x55] (the offset of the matching CCK register).
- Word 0xB70 of the copy (0x310), rewritten with sprE at every end of
  transmission while the latch is clear.

## Values the model does not reproduce

Against the captures (`08`), every word the ucode writes in the model and
the host then reads matches. 25 words the ucode never writes in the model do
not match, because the paths that write them depend on hardware the model
does not have:

- statistics accumulators and counters (0x3B4–0x3C5, 0x3ED–0x3F2), fed by
  SPRs of the IFS block or by RX/TX events;
- the background noise measurement (0x046, 0x184–0x18A, routine
  0x1208–0x1246): it starts on a MACCMD BGNOISE request, which the vendor
  tracer does not log, and samples real PHY registers;
- event counters (0x086, 0x087, 0x0A7) in the handlers at 0x0DE8, 0x09A3,
  0x09D9.

## Method

- The DSL-3580L captures have no `sel` on the object accesses: `lockstep`
  does not apply their SHM traffic.
- vd625 has no captures of its own; agcombo (same SDK line, another board)
  is a proxy.
- The ELF files are not in the archive: what is derived from them
  (versions, dates, symbols) is not checked again.
- In `08` the row of reads of words the host or the initvals wrote (41,854 /
  49,929 on D6220) cannot be reproduced with the published `lockstep.py`,
  which compares with the trace only the words the ucode wrote
  (`handoffs`, the 88 / 88 row).
